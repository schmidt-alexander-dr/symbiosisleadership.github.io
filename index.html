Symbiotic Leadership

The change I seek to make

For decades, we've treated economic growth and planetary health as separate ledgers. Buy now, pay later. But the bill is arriving. Disrupted supply chains, volatile energy markets, extreme weather, and a generation of leaders who sense that something fundamental needs to shift.

Symbiotic Leadership means recognizing that none of us will be well if the planet is not. It's a shift from extractive to generative, in how we think, lead, and create value.

This isn't a call to renounce ambition. It's a return to the original meaning of competition: com-petere, to strive together. The change starts ground-up, with leaders who have the humility to admit they don't have all the answers alone, and the courage to find them together.

How I work

Background

Built at the intersections

The Symbiotic Leadership philosophy grows from over a decade of applied work at the intersection of carbon accounting, supply chain ESG traceability, climate finance, and standards governance. I hold a PhD in Econometrics and have worked as an independent advisor bridging disciplines, institutions, and sectors. Those are the coordination problems that siloed expertise cannot solve.

That's what I bring to the leaders I work with now: a practical, integrated way of responding to problems that don't sit inside a single discipline.
